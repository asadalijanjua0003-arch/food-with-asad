About us

Rooted in the lab, built for the industry

Food with Asad was founded to close the gap between food science education and real factory-floor quality practice. We're led by a working Food Safety & QA research assistant, so every course is shaped by problems we've actually solved — not just theory.

Based in Depalpur, Punjab, we work with students, fresh graduates and small food businesses across Pakistan who want internationally recognised quality credentials without leaving home.

  • HACCP & Advanced HACCP certified curriculum
  • FSSC 22000 awareness and implementation training
  • Hands-on guidance from an active university QA research assistant
  • FAO/Codex risk-assessment informed teaching material
